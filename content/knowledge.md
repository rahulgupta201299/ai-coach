## Core frameworks the coach uses

### The CLEAR Goal method
- **C – Clarify**: What do you want, in one sentence? Why does it matter to you?
- **L – Look at reality**: Where are you today? What have you already tried?
- **E – Explore options**: List at least 3 possible paths, including "unrealistic" ones.
- **A – Act small**: Choose one action you can finish in the next 48 hours.
- **R – Review**: At the end of the week, what worked, what didn't, what's next?

### The 3-Bucket Priority filter (for overwhelm)
Write every task down, then sort into:
1. **Must** – real consequence if not done this week.
2. **Should** – important but can move.
3. **Could** – nice-to-have; drop or delegate.
Rule of thumb: no more than 3 "Must" items per day.

### Stay-or-Leave career reflection
Score each from 1–10: growth, people, pay, meaning, energy after work.
- Total under 25 → seriously explore a change.
- 25–35 → identify the one lowest score and try to fix it first.
- Over 35 → the issue may be temporary; look at what changed recently.

### Confidence ladder
Self-doubt reduces through evidence, not affirmations. Each week:
1. Write 3 things you handled well (however small).
2. Do one "slightly uncomfortable" action (speak once in a meeting, ask one question, share one idea).
3. Notice the story you told yourself before vs. what actually happened.

### Energy audit (work–life balance)
For 5 days, note after each major activity: energised (+), neutral (0), drained (−).
Then: protect the (+) activities, batch the (0) ones, and reduce, delegate or set boundaries on the (−) ones.

### Saying no — the 3-step script
1. Acknowledge: "Thanks for thinking of me."
2. Decline clearly: "I'm not able to take this on right now."
3. Offer (optional): "I could help with X next week" — only if you genuinely want to.

## Frequently asked questions
- **How is coaching different from therapy?** Coaching is future-focused and action-oriented for people who are generally functioning well. Therapy addresses mental-health conditions and past trauma; for that, a licensed therapist is the right person.
- **How soon will I see results?** Most clients report more clarity within 2–3 sessions; meaningful behaviour change usually takes 6–8 weeks of consistent action.
- **Do I need to prepare for a session?** Come with one topic you want to work on and what a good outcome of the session would look like.
- **Is what I share confidential?** Yes. Session content stays between the client and the coach, except where someone's safety is at risk.
